-- Q07 [LEFT JOIN] 회원별 예약 수를 조회한다 (예약이 없는 회원도 0건으로 포함)
SELECT m.id AS member_id, m.name, COUNT(b.id) AS booking_count
FROM member m
LEFT JOIN booking b ON b.member_id = m.id
GROUP BY m.id, m.name
ORDER BY booking_count DESC, m.id;
