-- Q11 [집계] 멤버십 이용 기간별 가입 회원 수와 회원이 내는 평균 월회비를 구한다 (COUNT + AVG)
SELECT p.months,
       COUNT(m.id) AS member_count,
       ROUND(AVG(p.monthly_fee), 0) AS avg_monthly_fee
FROM membership_plan p
INNER JOIN member m ON m.plan_id = p.id
GROUP BY p.months
ORDER BY p.months;
